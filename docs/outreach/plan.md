# Link plan: what to send to whom first

Prepared 14 November 2026. Starting point (Search Console, last 28 days): every external link to realhunt.co comes from the owner's own sites, all to the home page. Goal for the first eight weeks: 10 to 15 links from sites with real readers, to inner pages, and a profile that journalists can find. Nothing is sent without Carlos's approval.

## Before the first message (day 0)

1. Deploy this branch and check the pages on the live site: /research and its five datasets, /press, /method, /editorial-policy, /for-ai, /embed.
2. Open one CSV and one .md from the live site, and paste one embed snippet into a test page.
3. Carlos reads /press and /editorial-policy and confirms every statement (see the pull request for the open questions).
4. Ask Search Console to index /research and /press.

## Weeks 1 and 2: the closest audience, by hand, five messages a day at most (priority A in targets.md)

| Who | What | Template |
|---|---|---|
| Cheap-house newsletters: 11 Houses in Europe, Cheap Homes Tokyo, Cheap Homes Across Italy, Cheap Houses in Scandinavia | Budget page plus the country card for their country | 3 |
| Americans-abroad sites and newsletters: Expatsi, Escape From USA, Americans Abroad, Expat on a Budget | "Can Americans buy in X" pages and the rules summary | 2 |
| The Local (Spain, Italy, Sweden) | The rule for their country and the cost range, with the news hook of the week | 1 or 2 |
| Euronews Business | Prices and costs, as a ready data story with the CSV | 1 |
| Spanish: Eroski Consumer, Trabajar por el Mundo | Spanish costs and budget pages | 4 and 3 |

Why these first: they already publish this subject every week, they are small enough to read their mail, and a link from them sends readers who want exactly what RealHunt has.

In the same two weeks, with Carlos's own accounts: source profiles on Qwoted, Featured and Source of Sources (template 6 for the replies), and the five CSVs on Kaggle with the licence and a link to each page.

## Weeks 3 to 6: priority B

- Sellers and agents: each week, write to the agents of the 20 best-scored new homes with the score badge (template 5). This is the only outreach that grows with the site, and each badge is a link from a property site in the country of the home.
- Guides that state a cost figure (Wise, Property Guides, Moving to Spain, Scandification, HelpMyCash): template 4. Lead with a difference between their number and ours.
- Regional papers for the regions that rank well (The Olive Press for Granada, Almería and Murcia; idealista/news and Fotocasa Life in Spanish): the regions page.
- US press that covers Americans buying abroad (Fortune, CNBC Make It, Business Insider, Kiplinger, loveMONEY): only with a hook, such as a new rule for foreign buyers or a fresh monthly figure.
- One original chart on r/dataisbeautiful and helpful answers on r/AmerExit and r/ExpatFIRE, from Carlos's own account, following each community's rules. No more than one post a month per community.

## Ongoing

- First week of each month: note the three figures that moved most on /research/prices and send them to the journalists who answered before. A monthly number is what turns one mention into a habit.
- When a country changes a rule for foreign buyers, update the page the same day and tell the two or three reporters on that beat.
- Keep a simple log (date, person, page offered, answer, link yes or no) in this folder. Stop writing to anyone who does not answer after one follow-up.

## What not to do

- No paid links, no link exchanges, no guest-post farms, no automated mailing.
- No message that asks for a link without giving a fact.
- No figure quoted without its date and sample size; small samples are said to be small.
- No contact with competitors' portals.

## How to tell it is working

- Search Console, Links: linking sites other than the owner's own, and links to pages other than the home page.
- Referrals in analytics to /research/*, /can-i-buy/* and /embed.
- Requests to the badge and card images from other sites (server logs: /badge/score/*.svg and /embed/*.svg with an outside referrer).
- Indexed pages in Search Console: the count should start to rise a few weeks after the first editorial links.
