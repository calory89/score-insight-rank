# Outreach email templates (English and Spanish)

Six short messages, each tied to one page. Not sent to anyone. Rules for whoever sends them:

- One person, one page, one reason. Read their last article on the subject first and name it.
- Fill every `{...}` with the figure shown on the page **that day** (the numbers change as homes are added and sold) and say the date.
- No follow-up more than once, seven days later, two lines.
- Never ask for "a backlink". Offer the fact; the link follows when the fact is used.
- Sign as Carlos de Ory, RealHunt, carlos@equinoxinvestmentgroup.com. No phone number.
