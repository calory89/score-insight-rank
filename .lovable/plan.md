

# 10 More High-Impact "WOW" Features — Wave 2

Building on the existing 10-feature roadmap (2 built, 8 remaining), here are 10 additional strategic features that create massive value and differentiation.

---

### 11. AI Renovation ROI Simulator
Interactive tool where users input renovation budget and type (kitchen, bathroom, full refurb) and AI estimates the post-renovation value increase, updated ROI, and payback period. Visual before/after value comparison.

**Value**: Product delight, conversion, differentiation from every competitor.

---

### 12. Investor Match Network
Anonymous matching system connecting buyers, sellers, and co-investors based on investment criteria, budget, and target markets. Users opt-in and get notified of compatible investors — like Tinder for real estate deals.

**Value**: Network effects, viral growth, platform stickiness.

---

### 13. AI Market Forecast (12-Month Predictions)
For each analyzed market/city, generate AI-powered 12-month price trend predictions with confidence intervals. Show bull/base/bear scenarios with visual charts. Based on historical patterns and macro indicators.

**Value**: Premium positioning, retention, perceived intelligence.

---

### 14. Property Comparison Tournament
Gamified head-to-head comparison where users swipe between two properties and pick the better deal. Aggregated votes create community-powered rankings. Drives engagement and generates valuable crowd data.

**Value**: Engagement loops, gamification, network effects, data generation.

---

### 15. White-Label Report Export
Generate branded PDF reports with customizable logo, colors, and agent info. Real estate agents and advisors can use RealHunt as their analysis engine and present reports to clients under their own brand.

**Value**: B2B revenue stream, viral distribution, premium tier justification.

---

### 16. AI Due Diligence Checklist Generator
Based on the property type, country, and identified risks, AI generates a country-specific legal and financial due diligence checklist — notary requirements, tax implications, visa/residency programs, hidden costs.

**Value**: Trust, depth, international investor utility.

---

### 17. Live Mortgage Calculator with Bank Rates
Integrated mortgage simulator pulling indicative rates by country. Users input down payment and see monthly payments, total interest, and cash-on-cash return. Compare buying cash vs. leveraged.

**Value**: Practical utility, conversion, time-on-site.

---

### 18. Property Timeline & Activity Feed
For each property, show a timeline: when analyzed, price changes, score updates, notes added, shared with others, memo generated. Creates a living history of user interaction with each deal.

**Value**: Retention, product depth, professional feel.

---

### 19. AI Exit Strategy Advisor
Given a property's data, AI recommends optimal exit strategies: hold and rent, flip after renovation, Airbnb, sell in X years. Each strategy shows projected returns and risk profile.

**Value**: Differentiation, actionable intelligence, premium feel.

---

### 20. Embedded Market News Feed
Curated real estate news by market — regulatory changes, new developments, tourism trends, tax law updates. AI summarizes relevant articles and links them to the user's watched markets.

**Value**: Retention, daily engagement, information hub positioning.
